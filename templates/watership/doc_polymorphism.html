Polymorphism

Basic example

$Person
&+string:name
&-int:age
@constructer()     
    &name = "John Doe"
    &age = 10
@constructer() 
@constructer(string:_name, int:_age) 
    &name = _name
    &age = _age
@constructer() 

@+set_age(int:_age)
    &age = _age 
@set_age

@+get_age()
    &return:age
@get_age
$Person

$Student:Person Student extends Person
&+int:grade
@constructor()
&parent:constructor() calls parent constructer
&grade = 90
$Student

Student:student
&print:{student:name}:'s grade is :{student:grade}

Expected output

John Doe's grade is 90
